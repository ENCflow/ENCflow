# test/bendsed — Bed-load direction correction in a 90° bend channel (bed-slope term + equilibrium secondary-flow term)

> English mirror of test/bendsed/README.md. The Japanese file is the master copy.

[日本語](../README.md)

This case verifies the bed-load direction correction added to the bed-load
Exner equation (`f_fluvial`) on 2026-10-17 (developer.md §84.3; stage 1 of
the meander-development extension docs/meander_plan.md; both in Japanese).
The bed-load vector is q⃗_b = q_b (t̂ + r n̂ − γ∇z); `fluv_slope = 1` adds the
bed-slope term (γ = √(τ*c/(μs μk τ*)), Hasegawa type) and `f_secflow = 1`
the equilibrium secondary-flow term (r = N* h κ, where κ is the streamline
curvature of the depth-averaged velocity field).

## Configuration

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Domain and grid | 91 m × 51 m, 91 × 51 cells (Δx = 1 m) |
| Terrain | A channel 10 cells wide: a 60 m straight reach eastward from the west end → a 90° circular arc of radius 25 m (centreline) → a 20 m straight reach to the outlet edge. Bed slope 0.002 (along the channel length); both banks are dry ground at bed +2 m. Generated by `make_init.py` |
| Configurations | left (turning north; outlet = north edge) and right (turning south; outlet = south edge) are exact mirror images by flipping the arrays vertically. Each with on (with correction) / off (without correction; control): 4 configurations |
| Inflow and outlet | Constant segment inflow Q = 6 m³/s into the 10 channel cells at the west end; free outflow at the outlet edge |
| Initial depth | 0.6 m in the channel; the banks are dry |
| Sediment transport | Ashida–Michiue formula, d50 = 1 mm, τ*c = 0.05, porosity 0.4, change cap 0.05 m, soil layer 10 m |
| Coefficients (on) | μs = 1.0, μk = 0.45, N* = 7 (no defaults are set pending a literature check, so they are specified explicitly here) |
| Time | dt = 0.05 s, 600 s (update every step). `save_*/state.dat` at the end |

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Check_bendsed.py` | In the arc reach (excluding 2 m at both ends), computes the difference between the mean Δz on the outer-bank side (n < 0) and on the inner-bank side (n > 0). (1) on: difference < −0.05 m (outer-bank scour, inner-bank deposition), (2) off: difference > 0 (control; without correction the inner-bank side erodes more), (3) mirror difference between left and right < 1e-4 m, (4) Δz of the dry banks is exactly 0 |

## Running

```bash
make
./Run.sh          # generate inputs → 4 configurations (about 1 minute each) → Check
./Run_MPI.sh 4    # MPI. The rule is that state.dat is byte-identical to the serial run
```

## Findings (2026-10-17; gfortran 13.3, -O2 strict math)

| Check | on | off (control) |
|---|---|---|
| Mean Δz, outer bank − inner bank (left = right) | −0.224 m | +0.030 m |
| Mirror difference max|Δz_left − flipud(Δz_right)| | 2.2e-5 m | 2.3e-11 m |
| max|Δz| of the dry banks | 0 | 0 |

- Without correction the depth-averaged flow accelerates on the inner-bank
  side, so the inner-bank side erodes more; with correction the sign reverses
  and the outer-bank side deepens (with the revision of §84.3 that evaluates
  the bed-slope term by direct differences on the edges, this became clear at
  −0.224 m, from −0.098 m with the first version using central differences).
- The mirror difference is larger with correction (2.2e-5), because the
  positive feedback of morphological change amplifies round-off differences;
  it is within the tolerance 1e-4.
- The segment-inflow cells (west end) receive no sediment feed and scour down
  through the 10 m soil layer (local scour at a point source; excluded from
  the statistics). The free-outflow face at the outlet transports at
  capacity, so the bed as a whole tends to lower.
- A quantitative comparison with the equilibrium transverse slope
  ∂z/∂n ≈ r/γ has not been done; it needs a long run with fixed banks
  (remaining item of §84.3).
