# test/bankspill — Onset of spilling into a bank pocket (check of time-step independence and record of the current limit)

English mirror of test/bankspill/README.md

[日本語](../README.md)

This test examines what happens when the water level in a channel rises slowly and water starts to
spill into a low bank cell adjoining the channel (a bank pocket). Its subject is the edge depth of the
continuity equation and its cap (`f_dry_head_cap` in `&list_enc`;
[Advection schemes](../../../docs/en/users_guide/advection.md) §5, developer.md §68.16 and §108).

In real-terrain computations (Step 3 and the supplement of tutorials/chichibu), the discharge
downstream changes like a step at the moment channel water starts to spill onto a higher neighbouring
cell, and the time and size of the step change with the time step. This test isolates that
situation in two pockets.

**At present, the onset of spilling into a pocket directly ahead of the flow (the cell on the outer
side of a bend) depends on the time step in ENCflow.** The test judges only what the current code
satisfies, and displays the dependence on the time step and the size of the step at the onset as a
"current limit" (not judged). Revising the cap on the edge depth is a development item; once the rule
is in, the displayed items will become judged items (developer.md §110.21 and §110.22).

## Setup

Grid 60 × 8, Δx = 200 m, slope 0.0015, n = 0.03. A one-cell-wide channel flows east, shifts one row to
the south-east at column 31 (a bend), and flows east again. Outside the channel is a terrace 25 m above
the bed. Only two terrace cells are made "pockets" 7.2 m above the bed.

| Pocket | Position | Relation to the flow |
|---|---|---|
| S (side) | (15, 3), north of the channel cell (15, 4) in the straight reach | the flow is parallel to the bank |
| H (head-on) | (31, 4), the cell on the outer side of the bend; directly ahead of the flow arriving from upstream, and also adjoining the channel cells after the bend | directly ahead of the flow; once water enters, it becomes a path that shortcuts the bend |

The discharge is given as a source in the upstream end cell. After a 60-minute run-up at 4,030 m³/s
(normal depth 5.2 m), it is raised to 7,921 m³/s (7.8 m) over 100 minutes, held for 20 minutes and
lowered again over 100 minutes (the run is 300 minutes). The normal water level crosses the pocket
ground from below to above and back. The velocity is 3.9–5.1 m/s (velocity head 0.8–1.3 m, Froude
number 0.54–0.58). The east side is a free outflow. The input is generated by `make_bankspill.py`.

| Configuration | File | Pockets | Time step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `param_wet_dt6.txt` | thinly wet from a source of 0.05 m³/s | 6 s |
| 2 | `param_wet_dt15.txt` | same | 1.5 s |
| 3 | `param_dry_dt6.txt` | dry | 6 s |
| 4 | `param_dry_dt15.txt` | same | 1.5 s |

## What is measured

The "onset of spilling" is the first record (every minute) at which the depth in the pocket has risen
1 cm above the film at the end of the run-up. The water level of the adjoining channel cell in the
record just before it is measured from the pocket ground ($`\eta - z_p`$; negative means that water
started to enter while the level was still below the ground). The adjoining channel cell is the
diagonal neighbour on the upstream side, (14, 4), for S (its level is 0.3 m higher than that of the
cell directly beside the pocket because of the bed slope), and the cell just upstream, (30, 4), for H.

Into a pocket directly ahead of the flow, water can climb by the velocity head. The upper bound is the
energy head upstream, $`\eta + u^2/2g`$ (Bernoulli; the level at a stagnation point).

## Check items (Check_bankspill.py)

Judged items:

1. At the end of the run-up the channel is in uniform flow (depth within ±1 % of the normal depth).
2. S: the water level of the adjoining channel cell just before the onset is within ±0.15 m of the
   pocket ground (spilling starts when the level reaches the ground).
3. S: the level and time of the onset are the same for time steps of 6 s and 1.5 s (below 0.02 m and
   within 1 minute).
4. H: spilling does not start before the energy head upstream reaches the pocket ground.
5. H: after opening (from 140 minutes until 20 minutes after the discharge starts to fall), the depth
   in the pocket is the same for time steps of 6 s and 1.5 s (below 0.02 m).

Recorded items (not judged):

6. H: the water level upstream just before the onset (for each time step) and its difference between
   the time steps.
7. H: the largest one-minute rise of the depth in the pocket at the onset, and the roughness of the
   one-minute change of the discharge at the downstream transect (largest second difference).
8. H: the largest value of the pocket level minus the energy head upstream, from opening until the
   discharge starts to fall.

## Current values (items 6–8; the current limit)

| | Wet pocket, dt 6 s | Wet pocket, dt 1.5 s | Dry pocket, dt 6 s | Dry pocket, dt 1.5 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water level upstream just before the onset at H (from the ground) | −0.16 m (116 min) | −0.03 m (121 min) | −0.05 m (120 min) | −0.03 m (121 min) |
| Largest one-minute rise of the depth in pocket H | 0.64 m | 0.53 m | 0.90 m | 0.52 m |
| Roughness of the one-minute change of the downstream discharge | 245 m³/s | 162 m³/s | 187 m³/s | 112 m³/s |
| Largest pocket level minus energy head upstream | +0.18 m | +0.19 m | +0.18 m | +0.19 m |

- **The pocket directly ahead of the flow opens earlier with a larger time step.** With a time step of
  6 s it opens while the water level upstream is 0.16 m below the pocket ground, and with 1.5 s at
  0.03 m below (a difference of 5 minutes). The present cap uses the velocity head of the velocity
  normal to the edge, but the velocity of a closed edge is reset to 0 every step, so whether the edge
  opens is decided by "the velocity the edge gains in one step" and depends on the time step. The
  transition at the threshold of the cap toward a wet receiver (whose width is proportional to the
  time step; [test/bankcap](../../bankcap/en/README.md)) acts in the same direction.
- **At the moment of opening, water enters the pocket all at once** (0.5–0.9 m in one minute; on a
  200 m cell this is 20,000–40,000 m³). The condition for opening (the velocity head when the edge
  velocity is almost 0) and the level at which the pocket settles after opening (near the energy head
  upstream) do not agree. The downstream discharge shows a roughness of 100–250 m³/s per minute.
- **After opening, the pocket level exceeds the energy head upstream by 0.2 m.** This is slightly
  above the stagnation level (the upper bound), and it goes together with the edge depth being the
  mean of both sides (the mean of the deep channel and the shallow pocket), which makes the shortcut
  flow through the pocket large.
- The pocket beside the straight reach (S) does not show this problem (items 2 and 3).

## Limits and how to read the results

- Only a one-cell-wide channel and one-cell pockets are examined. With a subgrid channel
  (`fn_width`), the width of the transition at the threshold grows by the inverse of the plan-area
  fraction and the step becomes larger (in the 200 m run with a 50 m wide channel in the supplement of
  the tutorial, the pocket opened while the level was 1.1 m below the bank and gained 1.5 m in one
  minute). That configuration is not included.
- How much the water surface rises at the outer bank of a bend (centrifugal force, stagnation) cannot
  be resolved by a one-cell-wide channel. The test only checks that the upper bound (the energy head)
  is not exceeded.
- The recorded items are not judged, so passing this test does not mean that the time and discharge
  of the onset of spilling are correct. When a step affects how you read the results, halve the time
  step and check.
