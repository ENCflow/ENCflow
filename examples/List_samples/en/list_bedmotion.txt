! Prescribed bed motion settings sample (&list_bedmotion; developer.md
!   Sec. 70, docs/bedmotion_plan.md)
!   Enabled by fn_bedmotion = "this file" or "-" in the main parameters.
!   Forces z by a time history of ground displacement (snapshot table +
!   cumulative displacement rasters). h is unchanged, so the water surface
!   e = z + h is lifted (the generation mechanism of fault tsunamis).
!   Applied incrementally, so it coexists with geomorph and lavaflow.
!   Cells of the sea mask do not move (compute tsunamis with the "solved
!   sea", f_htype=2). No private state in restarts.
!======================================================================
&list_bedmotion
  f_bedmotion = 1           ! 0 temporarily disables while keeping the file
  fn_bmlist = 'bmlist.txt'  ! snapshot table: one line "time filename" (relative to
                            !   dir_data. Times are relative to t0, ascending. Each file
                            !   is the cumulative displacement d (m) over the whole
                            !   domain, in the same format as z. Before the table the
                            !   first value is held, after it the last)
  !f_bminterp = 1           ! time interpolation (1: linear, 0: step)
  !bm_tscale = 1.0          ! unit of the table times (s. 60 for minutes)
/
