! Wave conditions &list_wave (all parameters)

!   Name this file in &list_sysparam as
!     fn_wave = "list_wave.txt"
!   (without fn_wave the wave effects are completely inactive: no memory,
!   no CPU). Longshore transport rides the bed-load path, so f_fluvial = 1 in
!   fn_geomorph is required. Design: docs/wave_plan.md, decision: developer.md
!   §84.15, usage: docs/users_guide/geomorph.md "Wave-driven longshore transport".
!
!   Waves are not solved. The offshore wave (significant height, period,
!   direction of travel) is given; in the surf-zone cells the shoreline
!   orientation is taken from the depth gradient and the deep-water CERC
!   formula (Ashton & Murray 2006) gives the longshore transport, applied as
!   shore-parallel edge fluxes to the bed.
!
!======================================================================

  ! ---- time series (time, H0 (m), T (s), direction (deg); linear interpolation, end values held) ----
  !   time in minutes; with wave_clock = 1 (default) in morphological time (= hydraulic

  !wave_val(1:4,1) = 0,    0.5, 5.0, 90.0

  !wave_clock = 1
