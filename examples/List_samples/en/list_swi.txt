!
! Example of Soil Water Index settings &list_swi
!
!   Use this file by specifying, in the system parameters &list_sysparam,
!     fn_swi = "list_swi.txt"
!   (if fn_swi is not specified, the feature is completely inactive).
!
!   Computes the Soil Water Index (SWI) used by the JMA for sediment-
!   disaster warnings, with the three-stage series tank model of
!   Ishihara and Kobatake (1979) and the nationwide standard constants.
!   The tank constants are fixed (not changeable) to keep the official
!   definition.
!
!   Contract (users_guide/swi.md, developer.md Sec. 49):
!     - dedicated run: the only feature that may be combined is the
!       fixed-ratio interception (f_icmodel=1). Enabling any other
!       process module stops the run
!     - dt <= 600 s (the JMA computation interval of 10 min). Larger
!       values stop the run
!     - dx,dy are arbitrary (a warning that the conditions differ from
!       the official product is issued unless 1 km)
!
!   Output is automatic: Swi (distribution), Swi9999 (period maximum),
!   Swit9999 (time of the maximum)
!
!======================================================================
&list_swi
  ! Temporary disabling (0 with fn_swi still written = completely inactive)
  f_swi = 1

  ! Initial storage of the tanks S1, S2, S3 (mm) (effect of antecedent
  ! rainfall. Default 0 = dry start. Not needed across suspend/restart,
  ! which continues exactly from swi.dat)
  swi0 = 0.0, 0.0, 0.0
/
