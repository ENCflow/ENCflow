# ENCflow Name and Trademark Policy

*This English version is the authoritative policy. A Japanese translation is available in [TRADEMARKS.ja.md](TRADEMARKS.ja.md).*

ENCflow is open-source software intended to be used widely and freely in research, education, engineering practice, commercial activities, and other fields.

The ENCflow source code is distributed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Subject to that license, you may use, modify, redistribute, and commercially use the software.

The **ENCflow** name and official ENCflow logos, however, identify the official ENCflow project and its releases. This policy explains how they may be used.

The purpose of this policy is **not to restrict the use of ENCflow**. Its purpose is to preserve a clear distinction between the official ENCflow project and third-party modifications, products, services, analyses, or publications.

> **Use ENCflow freely. Modify it freely. Use it commercially.  
> Just do not present third-party work as an official ENCflow release, service, certification, or endorsement.**

## 1. Uses that do not require permission

You may use the ENCflow name, without asking for permission, to truthfully describe your relationship with the software. Examples include:

- using ENCflow for research, education, engineering analysis, consulting, or other work;
- referring to ENCflow in papers, reports, presentations, books, websites, teaching materials, or other publications;
- saying that a calculation, analysis, or study was performed using ENCflow;
- describing software, data, workflows, or services as being compatible with, connected to, or based on ENCflow;
- creating and publishing tutorials, courses, examples, utilities, pre-processing or post-processing tools, or other materials for ENCflow;
- providing paid or unpaid consulting, analysis, training, technical support, or other services that use ENCflow;
- modifying ENCflow for internal research, operational, commercial, or other purposes;
- redistributing ENCflow or modified versions in accordance with the Apache License 2.0; and
- describing derivative software as, for example, “based on ENCflow” or “an extension for ENCflow,” when that description is accurate.

Commercial use does not, by itself, require separate permission from the ENCflow project, and this policy is not intended to impose trademark royalties or additional software licensing fees.

## 2. Avoid confusion with the official ENCflow project

Please do not use the ENCflow name or official logos in a way that is likely to make users believe that a third-party product, service, release, analysis, organization, or publication is provided, approved, certified, or endorsed by the official ENCflow project when that is not the case.

Examples of uses that should be avoided include:

- distributing a substantially modified version simply as “ENCflow” without making the modification clear;
- using names such as “Official ENCflow,” “ENCflow Official,” or similar wording for an unofficial product or service;
- claiming “ENCflow certified,” “ENCflow approved,” or similar status when no such certification or approval has been granted;
- implying a partnership, sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement by the ENCflow project when none exists;
- using an official ENCflow logo as the primary logo of an unrelated organization, product, or service; or
- presenting results, model choices, parameter values, assumptions, or interpretations produced by a third party as if they had been reviewed or guaranteed by the ENCflow project.

## 3. Modified and derivative versions

The Apache License 2.0 permits modification and redistribution of ENCflow subject to its terms.

When publishing or distributing a modified version, we encourage you to use a name that clearly distinguishes it from an official ENCflow release.

Examples of clear descriptive wording include:

- `ABC Model — based on ENCflow`
- `ENCflow-based XYZ system`
- `XYZ extension for ENCflow`

Names such as the following may create an impression of an official edition and should therefore be discussed with the ENCflow project before use:

- `ENCflow Professional`
- `ENCflow Enterprise`
- `ENCflow Official Plus`

When the ENCflow name appears in the name or prominent branding of a modified distribution, we recommend including a clear notice such as:

> This is a modified version of ENCflow and is not an official release of the ENCflow project.

## 4. Products and services

You are welcome to provide commercial or non-commercial products and services that use ENCflow.

Examples include:

- flood, hydrological, geomorphological, or other analyses performed with ENCflow;
- consulting services using ENCflow;
- preprocessing, post-processing, GIS, visualization, AI, or cloud tools that work with ENCflow;
- systems that embed or call ENCflow;
- training courses and technical support for ENCflow; and
- model-coupling systems using ENCflow through interfaces such as BMI.

You may truthfully state, for example:

- “This service uses ENCflow.”
- “Compatible with ENCflow.”
- “Based on ENCflow.”
- “Couples with ENCflow.”

Such statements should not imply that the third-party product or service is itself provided, certified, or endorsed by the ENCflow project unless that is actually the case.

## 5. Research and education

This policy is not intended to restrict ordinary academic, scientific, educational, or documentary use of the ENCflow name.

You may use the ENCflow name normally in journal articles, conference presentations, theses, dissertations, reports, lecture materials, course exercises, websites, and similar works.

If you use ENCflow in research, please see [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) for citation information.

Results produced using ENCflow remain the responsibility of the person or organization that configured, executed, interpreted, and reported the calculation. Use of the ENCflow name does not mean that the ENCflow project has reviewed or guarantees the data, model choices, parameters, assumptions, results, or interpretations of a particular study.

## 6. Official logos

If official ENCflow logos are published, they may generally be reproduced unchanged when referring to, teaching about, linking to, or discussing the official ENCflow project.

Please do not:

- modify an official logo and use it as the identity of an unrelated product or organization;
- use an official logo in a way that suggests that a third-party product or service is an official ENCflow product; or
- use an official logo to imply certification, sponsorship, partnership, or endorsement that has not been granted.

## 7. Product names, organization names, and domain names

Using “ENCflow” as a prominent part of a third-party company name, organization name, product name, service name, or domain name may create confusion with the official project.

If you plan such use, please contact the ENCflow project in advance.

Ordinary descriptive references to ENCflow in page titles, documentation, compatibility statements, and explanatory text do not require permission.

## 8. Relationship to the Apache License 2.0

The Apache License 2.0 governs the copyright license for the ENCflow source code.

This policy concerns the use of the ENCflow name and official project identity. It is not intended to add restrictions to the rights granted under the Apache License 2.0 to:

- use the source code;
- modify the source code;
- redistribute the source code; or
- use the software commercially.

The main concern of this policy is avoiding confusion about **source, affiliation, certification, approval, or endorsement**.

## 9. Current status of the name

This policy states how the ENCflow project asks the ENCflow name and official project identity to be used. It does not state or imply that “ENCflow” is a registered trademark in any particular jurisdiction.

If the legal status of the ENCflow name changes in the future, this policy may be updated accordingly. Such a change will not alter the open-source license of the ENCflow source code.

## 10. Questions

Ordinary research, educational, engineering, and commercial use of ENCflow does not require advance permission.

If you are unsure whether a proposed name or presentation could imply an official relationship with the ENCflow project, please contact us through the project’s official GitHub Discussions or other official contact channel.

Our goal is to make ENCflow as widely usable as possible while keeping the identity of the official project clear.
